The problem

Our town is online.
Our shops aren't.

People already search on their phones. Today that search sends them to big outside platforms — while the shop down the road, the electrician next street and the auto driver at the junction stay invisible.

Before

  • ✕Local shops have no online presence
  • ✕Finding a plumber means asking around on WhatsApp
  • ✕Skilled workers wait for word-of-mouth jobs
  • ✕Apps only in English, built for big cities

With Local Link

  • ✓Every shop gets a storefront, orders and chat in minutes
  • ✓Services booked with a price and a time slot
  • ✓Workers listed with skills, rates and ratings
  • ✓Speak in Malayalam — the app answers by voice

Vision & mission

Filling the gap
to the AI future

Shopping has moved from walking in, to checking online, to asking AI. Local shops are still in the first step. Our mission is to carry them to the third.

Mission: make local businesses, services, people and useful information easy to discover and connect with.
🏪
Yesterday

Physical visits

Customers walk in, ask the shopkeeper, and buy. Trust is local — reach stops at the street.

📱
Today

Checking online

People search on their phones first. Local shops with no app or website simply don't appear.

THE GAP
🤖
Tomorrow · Local Link

AI platforms

Customers ask an AI in their own language. Local Link answers with their own town's shops — in Malayalam or English.

The problem

Your customers are online.
Your shop isn't.

🔍

Limited digital visibility

Customers know a shop exists, but not its products, offers, timings or contact.

🧩

Information is scattered

Phone contacts, WhatsApp groups, social pages and word of mouth — nothing in one place.

💸

Tech is out of reach

A website, app or AI system is too costly and complex for a small business to build.

Product roadmap

From Kozhikode
to every locality

Expansion-ready from day one: District → Panchayath / Municipality → Area.

PHASE 0

Validation

Vyapari meetings, merchant interviews, pilot area, institutional outreach.

PHASE 1

Foundation

Architecture, login, roles, locations, categories, API, design system.

PHASE 2

Customer MVP

Home, explore, search, profiles, services, offers, local info.

PHASE 3

Partner MVP

Registration, verification, products, offers, enquiries.

PHASE 4

Engagement

Messaging, notifications, reviews, favourites, news & events.

PHASE 5

Commerce

Cart, checkout, payments / COD, order status, delivery.

PHASE 6

Monetisation

Subscriptions, premium visibility, ads and campaigns.

PHASE 7

Scale

More districts, worker marketplace, classifieds, delivery.

PHASE 8

Intelligence

AI local search, recommendations and the voice assistant.
